About Me

I am a Computer Science student at Lagos State University (LASU), deeply passionate about the intersection of technology, security, and finance. As a Cybersecurity Analyst and AI/ML enthusiast, I am driven to build secure, innovative, and inclusive digital experiences. My skills in Python, Java, and React, combined with a foundation in Ethical Hacking, allow me to approach problems with a comprehensive and security-first mindset.

My journey has been enriched by roles such as a Fintech Ambassador for leading companies like Cleva and Cowrywise, and as a delegate for the Harvard Project for Asian and International Relations (HPAIR), where I've honed my leadership and collaboration skills. These experiences have provided me with a unique perspective on the real-world applications of technology and the importance of community building.

I am always eager to learn, grow, and connect with others who are passionate about using technology to make a positive impact. Let's build the future together.
